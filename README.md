## About The Greek Directory

The Greek Directory™ (TGD) is a growing digital directory and community hub connecting people with Greek-owned businesses and Greek-affiliated organizations throughout the United States. Its purpose is to make Greek America easier to discover and more connected by bringing the businesses, institutions, events, media, and resources that sustain Greek communities together in one place.

Founded on **November 8, 2025**, TGD began in **Chicagoland, Illinois**, with a simple idea: finding and supporting the Greek community should be easy. Greek-owned businesses, parishes, schools, cultural organizations, and community resources are part of everyday life across America, yet information about them is often scattered across separate websites, social media pages, and word of mouth. TGD brings these connections into one organized destination.

TGD's initial focus is on Greek life in America and the wider Greek diaspora: helping people support Greek-owned businesses, find a parish or school, discover an organization, take part in community life, and connect with useful resources. Its scope is intentionally open-ended and community-led. The Directory can grow to include anything the community needs or wants, and its offerings may evolve as those needs and interests change over time. Its current focus is community connection and resources rather than tourism, but its long-term direction is not limited to any one category.

## What TGD Brings Together

The following are examples of the areas TGD can include as it grows:

- **Businesses and professionals:** Restaurants, cafés, bakeries, markets, grocers, specialty imports, shops, professional firms, and local services.
- **Faith and community:** Greek Orthodox parishes and churches, religious organizations, cultural and fraternal associations, nonprofits, and community institutions.
- **Education and culture:** Greek schools, language learning, music, arts, dance, heritage programs, and educational resources.
- **Events and connections:** Festivals, parish celebrations, cultural gatherings, lectures, workshops, networking opportunities, and community events.
- **Media and information:** Greek-American news, publications, media outlets, useful links, and resources for people throughout the community.

The goal is broader than a list of businesses. TGD is intended to be a place where individuals, families, business owners, organizations, and communities can be found, supported, and connected. As it grows, the Directory will bring more of the Greek-American experience into one accessible resource, while extending its reach to Greek communities and the diaspora around the world.

## Our Mission

To connect and strengthen Greek communities by making Greek-owned businesses, organizations, institutions, events, and resources easier to find and support. TGD is built around community participation: every listing, update, recommendation, and shared resource helps create a more complete picture of Greek life and the people who make it thrive.
